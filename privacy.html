Privacy Policy

Personal Post Planner · Effective date: November 10, 2026

Personal Post Planner ("the App") is a private, self-hosted social media scheduling tool. It is operated by a single individual for managing their own social media accounts and is not offered to the public. This policy explains what data the App handles and how.

1. Information we collect

When you connect a social media account (such as TikTok) to the App, we receive, through that platform's official API and only with your permission:

  • Basic profile information: account ID, display name, username, and avatar.
  • Profile statistics: follower, following, likes, and video counts.
  • A list of your published videos and their basic metadata.
  • Access and refresh tokens that let the App publish content you schedule.

We also store the posts, captions, and media files you create in the App for scheduling.

2. How we use information

  • To publish posts and videos you have scheduled, at the time you choose.
  • To display your connected account and basic statistics inside the App.

We do not use your data for advertising, profiling, or any purpose other than operating the App.

3. Storage and security

All data is stored locally on the operator's own computer, in a private database that is not accessible from the internet. Access tokens are used only to communicate with the official APIs of the connected platforms.

4. Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your data with any third party. Content is sent only to the social media platform you choose to publish to.

5. Data retention and deletion

Data is kept only as long as the account remains connected. You can disconnect an account at any time inside the App, which deletes its stored tokens, or revoke the App's access from your platform settings (for TikTok: Settings and privacy → Security → Manage app permissions). To request deletion of any remaining data, contact us using the address below.

6. Children

The App is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their data.

7. Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes take effect when posted on this page.
